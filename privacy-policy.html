Snapfit Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

Snapfit ("the app") is a puzzle game made by Nuvyte. This policy explains what information the app handles.

Information we collect

None. Snapfit does not collect, store on our servers, or share any personal information. The app has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, and no third-party tracking.

Information stored on your device

Your game progress (levels completed, stars, points, trophies, album and settings) is saved only on your device so you can continue where you left off. We cannot see it. If you use Android's device backup, your progress may be included in that backup, which is controlled by you and Google, not by us.

You can delete your progress at any time in the app under Settings, Progress, Reset, or by uninstalling the app.

Internet access

Snapfit works fully offline. It does not send any data over the internet.

Children

Snapfit is suitable for all ages. Because the app collects no personal information, it collects none from children either.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
