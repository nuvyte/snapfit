Snapfit

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

Snapfit ("the app") is a puzzle game made by Nuvyte. This policy explains what information the app handles.

Summary

Snapfit has no accounts. We never ask for your name, email address, contacts, photos or precise location. The app uses Google Analytics for Firebase to send usage statistics so we can see which levels are too hard and improve the game. From version 1.1.0 the free game shows ads from Google AdMob, and you can buy a one-time "Remove ads" option through Google Play.

Usage statistics (Google Analytics for Firebase)

While you play, the app sends events such as: a level was started, finished or left; how many stars, hints, undos and seconds it took; the score; which screens were opened; trophies unlocked; and settings changed (sound, vibration, theme). Firebase also records basic technical details such as the device model, Android version, app version, language, country, and a random app-instance identifier created by Firebase.

This information is not linked to your name or to any account, and we do not use it to identify you. Analytics also reads your device's advertising ID, only so we can tell whether one of our own ads for Snapfit (for example on Google Ads) brought you to the game. We do not use these statistics to show you personalised ads. We also record when an ad was shown and its estimated revenue, so we can see whether ads pay for the game. Data is sent encrypted (HTTPS) and processed by Google as our service provider under the Firebase privacy and security terms. Google Analytics keeps event data for up to 14 months.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, Snapfit does not allow Analytics to store the app-instance identifier on your device or to use your advertising ID, so your events cannot be connected to each other over time or to our ads.

Ads (Google AdMob)

Snapfit shows ads from Google AdMob: a video you can choose to watch for an extra hint, and now and then a full-screen ad between levels. To show and measure ads, AdMob collects information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from it, device and app details, and how you interact with the ads. Google uses this as described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app shows Google's consent message before any ads load, and you can change your choice at any time under Settings, Ad privacy. Anyone can reset or delete their advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in Android settings (Google, Ads, or Privacy, Ads).

We ask Google to keep the ads suitable for a general audience.

Purchases

If you buy "Remove ads", Google Play handles the payment. We never see your card or payment details. The app only asks Google Play whether your Google account owns the purchase, so it can switch ads off and restore the purchase after a reinstall.

Update check

When the app starts, it downloads a small file from our website (nuvyte.github.io) to see whether a newer version is available. No personal information is sent with this request.

Information stored on your device

Your game progress (levels completed, stars, points, trophies, album and settings) is saved only on your device so you can continue where you left off. We cannot see it. If you use Android's device backup, your progress may be included in that backup, which is controlled by you and Google, not by us.

You can delete your progress at any time in the app under Settings, Progress, Reset, or by uninstalling the app. Uninstalling also removes the Firebase app-instance identifier.

Playing offline

Snapfit works fully offline. Without an internet connection, nothing is sent, no ads are shown, and the game plays normally.

Children

Snapfit is intended for players aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Your choices

You can change your ad consent in the app under Settings, Ad privacy (where consent is required), and manage your advertising ID in Android settings. Because the statistics are not linked to you, we cannot look them up by name or email. Uninstalling the app removes its app-instance identifier, so anything sent afterwards cannot be connected to what was sent before. For any other privacy question, email us.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
