Snapfit Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

Snapfit ("the app") is a puzzle game made by Nuvyte. This policy explains what information the app handles.

Summary

Snapfit has no accounts, no ads and no in-app purchases. We never ask for your name, email address, contacts, photos or precise location. The app uses Google Analytics for Firebase to send anonymous usage statistics so we can see which levels are too hard and improve the game.

Usage statistics (Google Analytics for Firebase)

While you play, the app sends events such as: a level was started, finished or left; how many stars, hints, undos and seconds it took; the score; which screens were opened; trophies unlocked; and settings changed (sound, vibration, theme). Firebase also records basic technical details such as the device model, Android version, app version, language, country, and a random app-instance identifier created by Firebase.

This information is not linked to your name or to any account, and we do not use it to identify you. The app does not collect the Android advertising ID and does not use the data for advertising. Data is sent encrypted (HTTPS) and processed by Google as our service provider under the Firebase privacy and security terms. Google Analytics keeps event data for up to 14 months.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, Snapfit does not allow Analytics to store the app-instance identifier on your device, so your events cannot be connected to each other over time.

Update check

When the app starts, it downloads a small file from our website (nuvyte.github.io) to see whether a newer version is available. No personal information is sent with this request.

Information stored on your device

Your game progress (levels completed, stars, points, trophies, album and settings) is saved only on your device so you can continue where you left off. We cannot see it. If you use Android's device backup, your progress may be included in that backup, which is controlled by you and Google, not by us.

You can delete your progress at any time in the app under Settings, Progress, Reset, or by uninstalling the app. Uninstalling also removes the Firebase app-instance identifier.

Playing offline

Snapfit works fully offline. Without an internet connection, nothing is sent; the game plays normally.

Children

Snapfit is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children, and the usage statistics described above contain no name, contact details or advertising ID.

Your choices

Because the statistics are not linked to you, we cannot look them up by name or email. Uninstalling the app removes its app-instance identifier, so anything sent afterwards cannot be connected to what was sent before. For any other privacy question, email us.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
